VITAL Sports

A VR mental-training app for elite golfers on Meta Quest 3 — breathing, immersive tournament footage and visualisation in one short session, guided by live heart-rate variability.

  • Unity
  • Meta Quest 3
  • Biofeedback
  • Spatial UI
  • Sports performance

Overview

VITAL — Virtual Immersive Training And Learning — prepares athletes for pressure before they meet it. A personalised session opens on a course the player knows, recalls their best rounds there and walks them through the moments that matter.

Each micro-session combines paced breathing, 180° tournament video and guided visualisation, while a heart-rate-variability readout shows how calm the body really is. The team calls it belief coding: it isn't magic, it's neuroscience.

Challenge

Elite players have little time and less patience for wellness apps. A session had to be short, feel premium from the first screen and speak to them personally — their course, their score, their name.

The biofeedback had to help, not distract. A number that jumps around makes people anxious; the readout needed to read at a glance and reward steady breathing without turning calm into a game.

Biofeedback Pipeline

From the arm to the headset

Heart data streams live from a Polar sensor into the headset, so the session reacts to how the athlete's body is actually responding.

  • SensorPolar Verity SenseAn optical heart-rate sensor worn on the arm, measuring every beat.
  • StreamBluetooth LEBeat-to-beat intervals reach the headset in real time while the session plays.
  • HeadsetMeta Quest 3The Unity app turns the intervals into a vHRV score, updated as the athlete breathes.
  • FeedbackvHRV readoutOne number, a live trace and a resilience scale, always in view.

Process

  1. A personal welcome. Every session opens on a card addressed to the player, with its length and what to expect — no menus to learn.
  2. The course as a postcard. The venue arrives as a stamped postcard that recalls the player's best round there, setting up the visualisation.
  3. Breathing on the fairway. A box-breathing pacer floats over a dusk-lit golf course, with In / Hold / Out / Hold steps that light up in turn.
  4. Tournament moments in 180°. Broadcast footage plays on a curved screen in the scene while the vHRV strip keeps reporting underneath.
  5. Biofeedback at a glance. One number, a scrolling trace and a high-to-low resilience scale — readable in a headset without breaking focus.

XR Prototypes

A series of rapid mixed-reality prototypes for Meta Quest, built during XR Bootcamp — each one a quick test of a single idea: physics, generative sound, AI vision and spatial learning.

Prototype 01

Physics Playground XR

An interactive XR sandbox that turns complex physics concepts into playful, hands-on experiments. Designed for educational institutions, it brings a virtual physics lab to every classroom.

Meta SDKHand trackingScene understandingShapesUniTaskPrimeTween
  • Believable physics simulations are hard to get right
  • Hand tracking feels natural for brief interactions, but lacks precision for extended use
  • It works best when virtual and real spaces align — like a virtual table matching the real one

Prototype 02

Gravity Composer XR

An immersive sandbox for ambient generative music: place sound-emitting objects in 3D space and let them fall, swing, collide or orbit under simulated physics — their motion plays the music.

Meta SDKHand trackingScene understandingShapesFMOD
  • Pivoted from room-scale interaction to a constrained, focused placement area
  • Took inspiration from Reactable to guide the interaction design
  • Richer sound needs real synthesis — something FMOD isn't built for

Prototype 03

Reality Scanner XR

A "magic scanner" that identifies real objects with AI. What if your surroundings didn't just exist in XR, but actually shaped the experience?

Meta SDKCamera APIOpenAI VisionOpenCV for Unity
  • On-device OpenCV detection hit a wall fast — performance, accuracy and integration fell short
  • Pivoted to the Quest camera feed paired with OpenAI's Vision API
  • AI vision pairs beautifully with spatial computing — generative interactions can feel magical

Prototype 04

Health Scope XR

Turns real-world space into an interactive diagnostic lens: explore lifelike 3D anatomy, hear pathological sounds and ask an AI about symptoms or conditions — all in real time.

Meta SDKCamera APIGroq STT & VisionOpenAI GPTOpenAI TTS
  • Integrating diverse AI APIs is tough, but worth it for fluid XR experiences
  • Spatial learning combined with AI conversation opens up powerful educational tools
  • XR isn't just immersion — it makes complex knowledge intuitive and accessible

Prototype 05

Heart Viewer

A WebGL build of the touch-free heart viewer — made for mid-air hand gestures, it works with a mouse too. Drag to start, then turn the heart, slice it along the X, Y or Z axis and switch between 3D and X-ray views.

CalmXR

A calm-tech experience for smart glasses. Short guided sessions help you relax, refocus and let go of stress with spatial audio, gentle visuals and simple focus exercises. It gives you a small pocket of calm wherever you are: at work, on the move or at home.

  • Smart Glasses
  • Calm Tech
  • Mixed Reality
  • Spatial Audio
  • Meta Quest 3

The idea

Calm you can wear

Most wellbeing apps ask you to stop, sit down and look at a phone. CalmXR is built for glasses you already have on. A session starts with one tap, fits into three to ten minutes and takes place in the room you are in. Your surroundings dim and soften, but they never disappear.

Consumer smart glasses with a display are not quite here yet, so Meta Quest 3 stands in for them. Its colour passthrough gives the same view: the real world with a thin digital layer on top.

Through the lens

Made to sit on glass

Everything is designed to live on a lens. There is one centred column, large type and glassy buttons that let the world show through. One question sits above the orb and one action below it, so each screen reads at a glance.

The orb is the app's only visual signature. It greets you, listens, shrinks into a loader and sets the pace of your breathing during a session.

Project goals

  1. 01

    Prototype for tomorrow's glasses

    Build the core interaction and concepts for future smart glasses, using current Meta Quest 3 hardware as a stand-in platform.

  2. 02

    Design for always-on wearables

    Explore UX patterns for lightweight, always-available devices: calm interaction, minimal visuals and intuitive guidance.

  3. 03

    Test the hard-to-test

    Evaluate ideas that are difficult to try any other way, such as ambient awareness, continuous presence and subtle focus cues.

Session flow

One session, eight moments

Every screen is one short question or one quiet instruction, floating in the room at eye level. Nothing asks for more than a glance and a tap.

  1. 01

    Invite

    “What do you have in mind?”

    The iridescent orb waits in the room. One button starts a session and nothing else competes for attention.

  2. 02

    Sense the space

    “Sensing the space around you.”

    The glasses capture the room and analyse it, so the session is shaped by where you are.

  3. 03

    Check in

    “How are you feeling today?”

    Eight honest answers: anxious, tired, angry or distracted, or wanting to focus, relax, recharge or just be calm.

  4. 04

    Pick a length

    “How long would you like to practice?”

    Three, five or ten minutes. That's short enough to take between meetings or at a bus stop.

  5. 05

    Prepare

    “Preparing your personalized session…”

    The orb shrinks to a quiet loader while the session is put together from your mood, space and time.

  6. 06

    Focus

    “Stay focused”

    The room dims and a slow visual gives your eyes one place to rest. The countdown stays small.

  7. 07

    Listen

    “Listen to the audio guide.”

    A spatial voice guides the exercise. The waveform shows sound is playing without asking you to watch it.

  8. 08

    Return

    “Session complete. Take a moment to notice how you feel right now.”

    The room brightens back and the session ends on reflection, not on a score.

In the headset

Field tests on Quest 3

These are passthrough recordings from real rooms. During a session the room takes on a calm tint, and a breathing cloud with a small timer holds your attention in the middle.

Digital Human

A digital human pipeline that takes a real person to a fully rigged, real-time avatar — from body and face creation through to AI-driven facial animation and rendering.

  • 3D Design
  • Technical Art
  • XR Development
  • Digital Humans
  • Real-time 3D

Pipeline

Six stages take a real person to a fully rigged, real-time avatar — each backed by dedicated tools.

  1. 01
    Body Creation Base shape of the face and body.
    Character CreatorHoudini
  2. 02
    Face Reconstruction 3D reconstruction of the face from captured imagery.
    Reality Capture
  3. 03
    Mesh Retopology Geometry retopology and projection onto the base mesh.
    Wrap4D
  4. 04
    Cloth Creation Garment design and cloth simulation.
    Marvelous Designer
  5. 05
    Character Design Facial expressions, makeup, hair and animation.
    Character CreatoriClone
  6. 06
    Animate & Render AI-based facial animation and real-time rendering.
    OmniverseAudio2Face

R&D

AI-driven face & voice

AI-driven facial animation and voice synthesis produce lifelike facial expressions and human-like speech — the foundation for believable avatars and immersive interaction across gaming, entertainment, VR and voice assistants.

  • Facial recognition & 3D modeling
  • Emotion analysis for expression
  • Text-to-speech voice synthesis

Face Reconstruction

From photos to a 3D face

Image-to-3D reconstruction turns 2D photographs and video into detailed, accurate 3D representations of a human face.

  • Multi-view capture to dense point cloud
  • Lifelike 3D facial models from 2D source
  • Feeds graphics, VR and medical imaging

Mesh Retopology

High-poly to clean base mesh

Retopology rebuilds a dense scan as a simplified, optimized base mesh — clean edge flow that is efficient and ready for animation across games, animation, VFX and product design.

  • Simplified, optimized topology
  • Deformation-friendly edge flow
  • Detail projected back onto the base
Base Retopo

Character Design

Texturing & realistic render

Lifelike textures are authored and applied to the model, then rendered to achieve a high level of realism — for film, games, medical visualization and VR.

  • Skin, hair, makeup & garment materials
  • Physically based texturing & lighting
  • Rendering tuned for believable realism

Untitled World

An augmented-reality art project from 2021. Wireframe sculptures hang over rooftops, bloom between park trees, drift above a frozen river and coil inside an underpass — a sketch of a world where the hologram has left the phone screen and the city itself becomes the gallery.

  • AR Art
  • Holographic Installations

  • Concept Art

Manifesto

A world where people's perception of reality is changed. Augmented reality has gone beyond the phone screen. Artists realize the concepts of karma, fate, memory in different parts of the world as holographic installations.

A world where human creativity is elevated to the absolute. Only the one who generates concepts is demanded, respected, needed and loved. No one can complain anymore about the namelessness of a world where only soil accompanies mortal life.
